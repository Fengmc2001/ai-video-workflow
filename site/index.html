04 / Making the film

From the first idea to the final frame从选题到成片

The repository follows the same order as the film: decide what the story is about, get to know the characters, then work out what each shot needs.
仓库按实际制作顺序整理：先想清楚要讲什么，再写人物、对话和镜头，最后处理画面与声音。

01

Topic选题

Start with a disagreement people recognize.

先找一个生活中熟悉的分歧。

↗ 02

Characters人物画像

Give each person a reason to speak.

让每个人都有自己的想法和说话方式。

↗ 03

Story & shots剧情与分镜

Build the conversation, then choose the shots.

先写好对话，再安排动作和镜头。

↗ 04

Prompts提示词

Connect each shot to its cast, lines and references.

把人物、台词、参考图和镜头对应起来。

↗ 05

Refinement局部精修

Add a line or adjust a moment in context.

补一句动机，或调整一句话的语气。

↗ 06

Postproduction后期

Interpolate, upscale, edit the audio and assemble.

插帧、超分、原声精修，再合成成片。
